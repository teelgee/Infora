# W2 Context layer: the director's plan (revision 2)

Written 2026-10-05 by the W2 director, to `master-plan.md` (which wins on
grammar, shell, cast and dates) and `research/flows-context-tools.md` part A
(which wins on screen copy for these beats). Revised the same day against
`review/review-accuracy.md` and `review/review-viewer.md` under the producer's
rulings (§13). Build source: `demo/studio/src/walkthroughs/w2/`.
Composition: `W2-context-layer`.

**In plain words.** This video follows one question through Infora's project
library. Rachel Bowen, for the client, opens the library: the documents the
project has accepted, plus the reports it was given. She opens the ground
investigation report and sees that only its latest version is used by the AI.
She then opens a map of how the library's documents connect. The newly
accepted method statement arrives with three suggested links, and none counts
until she confirms it, which she does, one by one. Eleanor Price, the
consultant, starts a new document, and the documents those links point to are
offered to her first as sources. She asks a question in Ask and gets an answer
where every claim carries its source. She opens one source and reads the exact
passage, from the live version of the report; the old version, which said the
opposite, never reached the answer. Finally Rachel and Eleanor ask the same
question side by side. Both get true answers. Rachel's also draws on an
ecology report she can see. Eleanor's is narrower, and nothing on her screen
hints that anything else exists. The video is 117 seconds, silent, with a
narration script for a later voiceover.

## 1. Purpose and the workflow, end to end

**What the video must prove, in this order:**

1. **The project library is a governed set, not a folder.** It holds accepted
   deliverables, which join automatically on acceptance (the method statement
   `WA-EIA-MST-001 C01` is W1's ending), and reference material the owner
   loaded (five received reports). Only the live revision of a document is
   used for AI answers. The ground investigation report's v1 was superseded on
   Sep 01, 2026. It stays readable as a record and is out of the AI's reach.
2. **Every document feeds a living ontology.** "How the library connects"
   shows each library document classified (Discipline, System, Document type,
   Project stage, Work package) and linked by typed edges. When the accepted
   method statement arrives, three links are proposed to the documents it was
   built from (`derived from WA-GEO-GIR-004`, `derived from WA-ENV-BAS-003`,
   `references WA-WAT-FRA-001`). None is live until confirmed, and Rachel, as
   the project owner, confirms each one. The ontology organises the library
   and is never cited.
3. **Every new document starts from it.** Eleanor opens the document creator
   and files the new document under the `Land quality` work package. The
   "Sources this draft may use" list then puts the related library documents
   first, each with a plain reason. Two of those reasons are the links Rachel
   just confirmed. Nothing is pre-ticked: she chooses her sources before
   anything is drafted.
4. **Ask answers from the library, with a source on every claim.** Eleanor
   asks Q1 under the Planning & Environmental Lens. The "What ran" rail ticks
   through five stages. The answer arrives claim by claim, drawing on four
   library documents, each claim followed by its source pill. The footer
   reads `6 cited claims · Composed for this question · Planning &
   Environmental Lens v1`, then the built honesty line, held long enough to
   read.
5. **A source opens on its passage, from the live revision.** She clicks the
   `WA-GEO-GIR-004` pill on the perched-water claim. The source panel shows
   `WA-GEO-GIR-004 · v2` `● Live`, `Cited passage · page 14`, and the passage
   highlighted. The caption pays off chapter 1: v1 found no groundwater, and
   the answer never saw it.
6. **Access limits show as absence.** Rachel and Eleanor ask the same
   question, Q3 `What ecological receptors are recorded along Ashcombe
   Brook?`. Rachel (`▤ 7 sources`) gets four claims: two from Redgate
   Ecology's appraisal `WA-ECO-PEA-001`, then the trout and pathway claims.
   Eleanor (`▤ 6 sources`) gets the same trout and pathway claims, word for
   word, and nothing else. Her answer is true, on topic and narrower. Her
   screen has no greyed row, no count of hidden material, no "withheld" line
   and no mention of Redgate. The appraisal is simply not part of her world.

**Seats.** Rachel Bowen (RB, Trentmoor Energy, Owner) appears in chapters 1
and 2 and on the left of the split. Eleanor Price (EP, Calder & Hale, Member)
appears in chapters 3 to 5 and on the right of the split. Ask happens on Mon
Oct 05, 2026 at 02:10 PM (never shown). The creator beat is undated.

**The glimpse and close.** Eleanor's Ask, with answer 1 complete, the
transcript narrowed, and the source panel open on `WA-GEO-GIR-004 · v2 ● Live`
with the passage highlighted. This is exactly the state at the end of chapter
5 (frame 2666).

## 3. Coordinates and conventions used in the shot table

- Every point is given **native**, then **→ screen** on the 1920×1080 stage.
  Native means the 1536×864 plate, where content-column x is the 1440
  screenshot's x + 48.
- **Wide:** `sx = 120 + 1.09375·nx`, `sy = 24 + 1.09375·ny`.
- **Work/Detail** with key `(cx, cy, m)`: `sx = 960 + (nx − cx)·1.09375·m`,
  `sy = 496.5 + (ny − cy)·1.09375·m`. The cursor is authored native and rides
  the camera through `useAppPoint`.
- **Split:** left `sx = 120 + 1.09375·nx`, right `sx = 978 + 1.09375·nx`,
  both `sy = 60 + 1.09375·ny`, on a 752×832 shell.
- **Cursor:** travel time `T = clamp(15, 10 + d/45, 27)`, with `d` in screen
  px. The cursor arrives, dwells 8 f, clicks, then dwells 6 f. The UI responds
  at click + 2.
- **Typing:** `HumanTyped` with `seeded(seed)` (mulberry32, `motion.ts`).
  - The gap per character is `[1,2,2,2,3][floor(r·5)]`, plus 4 after a comma
    and 8 after a mid-string full stop. The first character lands on the
    `from` frame.
  - The end frames below were computed with that algorithm.
  - **If the lead's `HumanTyped` differs, take `endsAt()` and absorb the
    drift in the Working state that follows** (shots 4.4 and 6.4 carry ±8 f
    of slack).
- **Streaming:** `CitedClaims` reveals at 4 c/f.
  - Claim `i` ends at `start + ceil(len/4) − 1`.
  - Its pill fades in (`ui`, 5 f) at end + 2, and the next claim starts at
    end + 5.
  - A second pill on the same claim follows 3 f after the first.
- **Positions:** controls are the layout targets in §4. **The builder
  measures each rendered control's centre and moves the target onto it,
  keeping every frame number.**
