The three channels

grounded

cited as fact

The project's own library, filtered to the deliverables this organisation is entitled to, plus the authority corpus. This is what an answer's citations resolve to.

guiding

steers · never cited

The project's templates, style guides and guardrails. Shapes how something is written; never appears as a source.

informing

informs · never cited

The organisation's own context — its precedent, know-how and method. Private, portable, and it travels with its people into every project they work on.

The rule this rests on

A request runs in exactly one environment, and the scope is resolved from (project, requester) before any store is touched. There is no parameter that could widen it. So a person who works for two clients cannot bring one client's material into the other's project — the environment is the wall, not a rule someone has to remember.
