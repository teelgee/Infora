# ADR-0033 - Per-organisation deliverable plans with owner consolidation; appointments as auditable grants with propose-then-confirm; the roster is visible; organisation administration

**Status:** Accepted (owner-ruled 2026-07-18, Slice A design session; drawn in [diagrams/review roles.tldr](../diagrams/review%20roles.tldr)). Refines ADR-0008, 0015, 0017, 0019.

**Context:** ADR-0008 modelled one deliverable plan, and the appointment ADRs left the review group and authoring team as named sets with no stored shape. Real projects run the TIDP/MIDP structure: each producing organisation plans its own deliverables, and the client consolidates. The reviewer picker also forced two facts the access model implied but never stated: who a proposer can *see*, and who administers an organisation's own people onto a project.

**Decision:** **The deliverable plan is per-producing-organisation.** Each producing organisation keeps its own plan — the schedule of deliverables it will produce (the TIDP): it creates each container (which gains a `producing_organisation_id`), sets its coded reference and metadata, names its authoring team, and proposes reviewers where known. The **owner consolidates**: the single project schedule (the MIDP view) is the view over every organisation's planned containers; the owner curates access, monitors cycles, and maintains reviewer appointments. A plan needs no table of its own — an organisation's plan *is* its set of planned containers; the two plan surfaces are filtered views of one substrate.

**Appointments are auditable interval grants.** Two tables per deliverable: `deliverable_author` and `deliverable_reviewer` — container, participant, `is_lead`, granted/revoked timestamps, granted-by. Append-only intervals, no copy at cycle launch: the cycle consumes the live sets (ADR-0017's inherit-then-amend collapses to reading them), and "who could act in round N" is reconstructed from the intervals against the round's timestamps — no per-round membership snapshots. The producing side writes its author rows; reviewer rows may be **proposed** by the producing organisation at planning ("if known"), conferring **no access until the owner confirms** (`confirmed_by`/`confirmed_at`) — and confirmation is exactly where ADR-0015's invariant bites: confirming a reviewer whose organisation lacks the deliverable share forces the owner to share with that organisation first, or decline.

**The roster is visible: the wall hides work, never presence.** Every Participant sees the project roster — name, organisation, project role — of all Participants, which is what lets reviewers, authors and assignees be picked from a list. Nothing about any organisation's *work* is disclosed by presence.

**Organisation administration**, minimally: a User carries an `org_role` (`admin` | `member`). An org admin manages the organisation's own users and enrols or removes them on projects the organisation is a member of — the owner opens the organisation's seat (Membership, propose-then-confirm per ADR-0019, now carried as a `state`), each organisation staffs it (ADR-0015's two levels exactly). The access edges become auditable records: Membership gains `state`, added-by and timestamps; Participant gains added-by, `created_at` and a nullable `removed_at` — an interval, never a deleted row.

**Consequences:** Removing a departed Participant mid-cycle closes their interval and auto-revokes their grants from that moment, while every comment, response and code they wrote stays attributed and frozen in issued rounds — ADR-0017's past-rounds-never-altered is now enforceable because everything is intervals and nothing is deleted. Container creation and metadata are producing-organisation acts; the owner's MIDP shows every deliverable that should exist across all organisations. The reviewer picker never needs free text; proposals always reference roster Participants. ADR-0016's "owner grants all access" continues to mean cross-organisation access only; intra-organisation enrolment is each org admin's. Builds on ADR-0008, 0015, 0016, 0017, 0019, 0032.

---

**Addendum (owner-ruled 2026-08-06, T-352): the lead reviewer manages the review group.** The appointment authority as first cut was two-sided — the producing organisation proposes, the owner confirms (and an owner grant auto-confirms). Reviewing the built status page, the owner added the third party the model already trusts to run the round: **a live, confirmed lead reviewer may propose reviewers from the visible roster and may revoke reviewer rows**, from the deliverable's status page and anywhere else the acts surface. The wall shapes the split exactly as before: a lead-reviewer *proposal* is **non-confirming unless the proposer is owner-side** — confirmation stays the owner's act, because confirmation is where ADR-0015's share-first invariant bites and where access begins; a lead-reviewer *revocation* needs no such gate, because revocation narrows access and is always wall-safe. Nothing changes for the producing organisation or the owner; the addendum widens who may fire two existing acts and rewrites no consequence.
