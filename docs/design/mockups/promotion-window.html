Design decisions in this mockup
Reconfirms, never composes ADR-0008 made promotion near-empty on purpose, because a form filled in the moment is a form filled wrong. This window survives that objection by splitting the fields: the plan-owned identity block is shown and locked, so a thoughtless confirm produces the correct state, and only authoring metadata — which is already yours to edit in the sandbox — takes input.
Naming proposes; it does not grant Every reviewer control writes a proposal. Confirmation stays owner-side because that is where ADR-0015's share-first invariant bites — confirming a reviewer whose organisation lacks the deliverable share forces the owner to share first or decline. The producing organisation can never open the wall from this window.
The wait is stated before the act, not after Changing the lead reviewer stalls the round until the new lead accepts. State B says so in the window, with the cheaper alternative named. The system does not refuse the change — it is a legitimate act — but it will not let it be made blind.
The period is a guide with an owner at last Ten working days by default, set for the project beside the schema, adjustable here for one submission. The target date is shown computed so the number means something. It chases and flags; it never closes a round, which stays the lead reviewer's human act.
What promotes is named With the QA gate on, promotion has one legal source. The window says which version it is taking and who accepted it, because the whole point of the gate is that the client receives the version that was checked.
The removed reviewer, stated briefly State B strikes Rachel Bowen through and pills her Removed, and says no more. Her comments on round 1 stay attributed and frozen either way — the grant is an interval, not a delete — but spelling that out in a promote dialog is clutter, and it belongs on the review record where those comments actually are (ruled 2026-08-19).
