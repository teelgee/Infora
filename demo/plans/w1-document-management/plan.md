# W1 Document management: the director's plan

Written 2026-10-05 by the W1 director, to `master-plan.md` (which wins on
grammar, shell, cast and dates) and `research/flows-docmgmt-collab.md` part A
(which wins on screen copy and layout for these beats). Revised the same day
against `review/review-accuracy.md` and `review/review-viewer.md` (see the
revision log, §13). Build source: `demo/studio/src/walkthroughs/w1/`.
Composition: `W1-document-management`.

**In plain words.** This video follows one document through its whole
controlled life, using the real screens. Eleanor Price, at the consultant
Calder & Hale, links her draft to the slot her firm's plan already holds for
it. Her colleague Owen Vance checks it in-house first: he asks her to cite the
ground investigation report by its accepted version, and she does. Eleanor
then sends that checked version (P01) to the client, Trentmoor Energy, where
Rachel Bowen is the named lead reviewer. Rachel accepts the appointment, opens
the review and writes comments on the page itself. While she does, Calder &
Hale can see only that a review is happening, not what it says. Rachel sends
the comments back with the code S0 (work in progress, back for revision).
Eleanor answers every one: either "here is the passage that changed", or "no
change, and here is why". She sends the revised version, P02. Rachel reads
every comment beside its answer, sees nothing new was raised, and accepts with
A1. The contractual revision C01 is issued and the AI may rely on it from then
on. The video runs 127.9 seconds, is silent, and comes with a narration script
for a later voiceover.

## 1. Purpose and the workflow, end to end

**What the video must prove, in this order:**

1. A document's place in the project exists **before** it is written. The
   plan issues its reference; nobody types it.
2. Drafts live in the firm's own working area, and are linked (bound) to
   their planned slot. Binding gives the draft the plan's reference and title.
3. Nothing leaves the firm unchecked. The draft is frozen, a colleague checks
   it in-house, his comment is answered, and that check stays inside Calder &
   Hale. (The in-house code is never shown; the promotion window's `accepted
   by internal QA` carries the sign-off.)
4. Promotion sends **exactly the version that was checked**, with the plan's
   details locked, to a named, confirmed lead reviewer at the client.
5. The client accepts the appointment by name ("Only you close a round"), and
   only acknowledging opens the review.
6. Review comments are pinned to the words they are about, each with a code.
7. **The ethical wall holds during the review.** Calder & Hale sees that a
   review is open and nothing else, until the lead reviewer issues it.
8. One code (S0) closes a round. The comments go back to the authors.
9. **Comments are dealt with by both sides and closed out.** The author
   answers every comment, either pointing at the changed passage (which is
   really in P02) or saying "no change" with a reason. Nothing goes back until
   all are answered. The reviewer reads every answer (`3 comments · 3
   answered`) and raises nothing new in round 2.
10. One live record shows every comment beside its code and its answer.
11. **Authoritative:** A1 (authorised and accepted). The contractual revision
    C01 is issued and the amber "Not used for AI answers" badge goes.

**The deliverable:** `WA-EIA-MST-001`, `EIA Scoping Method Statement — Land
Quality`, producer Calder & Hale, discipline `EEN`, suitability `S2`,
`OFFICIAL`, `DOCX`. Working title before binding: `Land Quality scoping method
statement — working draft`. Path: v0.1 → P01 (round 1, verdict S0) → P02
(round 2, verdict A1) → **C01**. In every version its text says "the scheme"
(flows E-9) and puts the PAH hotspots **at Wrenfield Sidings** (accuracy
must-fix 3; W4's older working copy keeps the error its checker catches).

## 3. Coordinates and conventions used in the shot table

- **Native** = the app's CSS px at 1536×864 (master §2.2). Every native point
  was measured on the 1440-wide screenshot and converted:
  - content-column elements and centred modals: **x + 48**, y unchanged;
  - elements pinned to the pane's right edge (topbar badge, the editor's
    second-bar right group, the editor side panel): **x + 96**;
  - the rail: unchanged; the user chip: **y − 36** (the pane ends at y 852).
- **Screen** (1920×1080) from native, through `useAppPoint`:
  - **Wide** `(768, 432, m 1)`: `sx = 120 + 1.09375·nx`, `sy = 24 + 1.09375·ny`.
  - **Work / Detail** `(cx, cy, m)`: `sx = 960 + (nx − cx)·1.09375·m`,
    `sy = 496.5 + (ny − cy)·1.09375·m`.
  - Screen points in the table are rounded; the builder authors native points.
- **Cursor travel** `T = travelFrames(d) = clamp(15, round(10 + d/45), 27)`
  with `d` the screen distance at the camera in force. Every click = arrive,
  dwell 8 f, click (press 6 f, ring 12 f), UI responds at click + 2, dwell
  6 f before the next move. `→` reads "travel from–to (T)".
- **Typing** uses the built `typingSchedule` (`walkthroughs/shared/typing.tsx`).
  The seeds below were run through it, so the end frames are **exact**
  (`typedEndsAt`), not budgets: comment 1 seed 44 = 160 f; response 1 seed 44
  = 149 f; reason seed 63 = 143 f. If a string changes by one character, re-run
  the seeds. After the last character the caret blinks for a 6 f settle.
- **Cursor idle rule:** after > 90 f idle the cursor fades out (6 f) and back
  in 10 f before its next move. Fades are listed per shot.
- **Select menus** are native `<select>` controls (`packages/ui/src/form.tsx`
  165–185), drawn as the shared `Dropdown`: white, radius 8, overlay shadow,
  hairline, 28 px rows, 12.5 px text, hover `#efeeeb`, fade 4 f, 318 native
  wide (x 1051–1369), ellipsis truncation. Options in order: the placeholder
  (`no code` / `choose a code…`), then `WT_CODES` (§11.1): S0, S1, S2, S3, S4,
  S5, A1. In a **Deliverable verdict** menu the A1 row reads `A1 — Published —
  authorized and accepted (accepting)` (`cycle.tsx` 715–725). Under the
  comment-code select (y 573) the menu opens **down**: rows centred at y 612
  (`no code`), **640 (S0)**, 668 … 808. Over a low verdict select it opens
  **up** (bottom edge 4 px above the select); row centres are given per shot.
- **Camera moves** start ≥ 90 f apart, never while typing, travelling or in
  the split. Moves are counted (in §5.1) to the state they enter.
