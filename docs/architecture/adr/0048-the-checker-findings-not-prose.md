# ADR-0048 - The checker returns structured, anchored **findings**, not prose: it is a *checking* capability whose optional proposed fix rides the existing Suggestion mechanism and is never auto-applied; and a firm's rulebook stays **guiding** material that a finding may quote **verbatim but never cite** — quotable-but-uncited, org-private, and distinct in render from a grounded citation

**Status:** Accepted (owner-ruled 2026-08-01, design session 2 — `Q-007` and `Q-008` ruled together because they are one decision seen twice). Refines **ADR-0021** (the three channels) and the **2026-07-29 two-tier authority ruling** without reopening either. References **ADR-0011** (the library is the grounded corpus), **ADR-0022** (channel retrieval and the attached-source working set), **ADR-0027** (thinking vs doing), **ADR-0028** (Run/Step), **ADR-0030** (rails), and component 6 §6's answer invariants. Decided against [v1.md](../../build/v1.md)'s Phase C. **Amended 2026-08-30 (owner-ruled, the checker session — [ADR-0053](0053-the-checkers-run-and-the-executor-rule.md)), in two parts, neither reopening this ruling.** **(1) §4's channel name is corrected from *guiding* to *informing*.** This ADR placed a firm's rulebook on the guiding channel while asserting that nothing in ADR-0021 was amended, and both cannot hold: ADR-0021 maps org context to the **informing** channel and classifies **by ownership, not purpose** — a house drafting manual is the firm's own IP and leaves when the firm leaves the project. The substance of §4, §5 and §6 is unmoved, because *does not ground*, *quotable but never cited* and *private to its organisation* hold identically on informing; T-308's build is unaffected in access, its query keying on organisation with no wall predicate. What the mislabel cost was **precedence** and **drop order**: a project style guide is the guiding channel's definitional occupant, so the two style sources would have shared one bucket with nothing to rank them and ADR-0029's *guiding wins, firm know-how fills the gaps* would have been unreachable; and `mergeChannels` drops informing first, so the correction means a firm's rulebook is dropped **before** a project style guide — which is why ADR-0053 §5 makes the planner **split rather than drop**. The channel label in the code and its tests move with this correction. **(2) §3's no-bulk-accept rule is narrowed to *rewrites*.** The guard stands where it was aimed and not where it was not: a **rewrite** — model-written prose — is accepted one mark at a time, always, with no accept-all and no accept-all-of-one-rule. A **mechanical** fix — the same substitution applied consistently, expressible as find-and-replace and displayable in one line — **may be accepted in bulk**, and which kind a fix is is declared on the finding and checkable in code rather than self-certified by the model. The distinction §3 originally drew was the surface, not the act: in the editor and the Word add-in you are **drafting**, so every acceptance is a separate authorial act; in a checker, fifty-five identical capitalisations are one decision applied fifty-five times, and requiring fifty-five clicks buys no additional human check while producing the outcome `Q-031`'s stress test predicted — the consultant rejecting the tool rather than the marks. **Bulk *dismissal*** by rule, severity or check is likewise permitted and records no reason; it resolves findings rather than overriding ADR-0049 §7's gate. Reasoning in [ADR-0053](0053-the-checkers-run-and-the-executor-rule.md) §6.

**Context:** V1 ships three declared workflows, and **the checker is the only one that is a new capability rather than composition** — the thing that makes Infora a product rather than a chatbot. It reviews a draft against three inputs: the project **corpus** (grounded), **manually attached documents** (ADR-0022's working set), and the firm's own **style guide or rulebook**. Two questions blocked it, and the session found they were coupled.

*On `Q-007` (the rulebook's channel), the tracker's framing offered a fork the record had already closed.* The 2026-07-29 ruling settled that **placement does not set channel role** — an organisation-tier provision lives in org context and still grounds and cites, because the operative test is what the material **is** (a published external instrument), not who loaded it. So "org context versus the org tier" was never a choice about *where* the rulebook lives; both live in org context. The live question was whether a firm's rulebook passes that existing test, and it does not: a house drafting manual is the firm's own preference document, not a published external instrument.

A tempting middle position was considered and rejected during the session: that a rulebook *restating* IEMA guidance or an ISO clause thereby becomes groundable. It does not — **a restatement is still the firm's own document**. Where a rulebook points at an instrument the firm has separately licensed and loaded, the citable thing is that instrument, never the manual's paraphrase of it.

*And a V1 fact shrinks `Q-007` further.* [v1.md](../../build/v1.md) defers the authority corpus **in both tiers**, so in the pilot the organisation tier does not exist and no ISO or IEMA material can ground however this is ruled. The question that actually mattered for V1 was therefore not *which channel* — that is forced — but **what the checker may say when its only rulebook is guiding material**.

*On `Q-008` (the output shape), the risk runs the other way.* The Engine backlog has carried "checking-vs-generative and results presentation" as open since the components were re-cut. The pull is toward generation, because a checker that hands back a corrected draft demonstrates better than one that hands back a list — and that pull is exactly what the platform's liability model cannot afford.

**Decision:**

**1. A finding is a structured record, not a paragraph of prose.** The checker's output is a list of findings, each carrying: the **input it arose from** (corpus, attached document, or rulebook), an **anchor into the draft**, a **severity**, the **evidence** that supports it, and optionally a **proposed revision**. Prose is what a chat turn returns; a finding is what a reviewer can sort, filter, assign and clear.

**2. Anchoring is what makes a finding actionable, and it is not optional.** A finding that does not point *into the text* is a report, not a tool — the reviewer is left to locate the problem themselves, which is the friction that makes a checker feel like a toy. Findings anchor at the same locator grain the citation work establishes, which makes **locators (T-271) a hard dependency of the checker**, not an adjacent nicety.

**3. The checker is a *checking* capability. It may propose a fix; it may never apply one.** A finding's optional proposed revision **enters the existing accept/edit/reject Suggestion mechanism** that already produces Signals. This is the load-bearing part of the ruling: the checker gains **no write path that the drafting side does not already have**, so every change to a deliverable remains a human act performed through a mechanism that is already audited, already reversible and already understood.

Two constraints keep this from collapsing into generation:

- **Proposed revisions are collapsed by default and are never applied in bulk.** The moment findings arrive with fixes attached and an "accept all" beside them, reviewers stop reading findings and start accepting patches — and the human check the professional-liability model depends on (the 2026-07-12 ruling) quietly evaporates while appearing to be performed.
- **A finding states a defect, never a verdict.** Component 6 §6's first answer invariant applies unchanged: a finding says *"this paragraph asserts a 12% figure with no source in the library"*, never *"this chapter is non-compliant"*. The system does not give an unqualified professional opinion, and a checker is precisely where that boundary would be easiest to cross by accident.

**Rejected: the checker rewriting the section.** It would be the most immediately impressive demonstration and it collapses checking into drafting — the same run that judges the work would produce its replacement, leaving nothing independent to check. ADR-0027's thinking-vs-doing line and the answer invariants both exist to prevent this.

**4. The rulebook rides the guiding channel in its entirety.** It steers the checker's judgement; it is not a grounded source; it is never cited as project fact. Nothing in ADR-0021 is amended and the authority corpus stays out of V1 entirely.

**5. A finding may quote the rulebook *verbatim*, and that quote is not a citation.** This is the answer to `Q-007`'s stress test — *if the rulebook only steers, a finding cannot cite the rule it enforces* — and it is the third option the stress test itself hypothesised, not a carve-out from the channel model.

The reconciliation is precise rather than convenient. ADR-0021's guiding channel forbids material being **cited as project fact**. A house rule is not a fact about the scheme; it is an **instruction to the author**. So a finding may read:

> *Your Drafting Manual §4.2 says "avoid the passive voice"; this paragraph uses it three times.*

— quoting the rule that makes the finding intelligible, without asserting anything about the project and without the quote entering the citation register. **The render must differ from a grounded citation**, or the distinction survives in the model and dies on the screen: a quoted rule is not a source chip and must not resolve like one.

**6. A quoted rule is org-private, and this is a wall constraint rather than a preference.** The rulebook lives in org context, so a finding quoting **Org B's** manual must never appear on a surface **Org A** reads. Findings therefore inherit the producing-organisation-private posture the run record already has (the 2026-07-12 ruling), and the constraint binds the findings surface, not merely the checker. Leaking a competitor's internal drafting standards through a review comment would be an ethical-wall breach delivered by a feature nobody thought of as crossing the wall.

**7. What the checker grounds on is unchanged.** Findings arising from the **corpus** cite normally and resolve to a locator inside the source document; findings arising from **attached documents** follow ADR-0022 — the working set never grounds and is never cited; findings arising from the **rulebook** quote per decision 5. Three inputs, three honest treatments, one schema.

**Consequences:** The Engine's long-open "checking-vs-generative and results presentation" item closes, and component 6 §3 gains a settled position. V1 gains a **finding schema in `contracts`** that both the checker DAG and the findings surface depend on, which is why it is ticketed ahead of both. The checker acquires a hard dependency on locators (T-271) that it did not visibly have while it was unruled. The findings surface inherits an org-privacy obligation from decision 6 and a render obligation from decision 5 — a quoted rule must be visually distinct from a source chip. `Q-007` and `Q-008` close together; T-308, T-310 and T-311 unblock. Nothing in ADR-0021, ADR-0011 or the 2026-07-29 two-tier ruling is reopened — this ADR applies their existing tests rather than adding to them.

**Considered and not taken.** *Building the organisation tier into V1* so findings could cite real ISO/IEMA clauses — the strongest checker, and where the product ends up, but it pulls T-277's store and in-force interval model into the pilot and adds a standards-licensing conversation on top of the DPA the pilot already needs. *Findings that describe a breach without quoting the rule* — the strict reading of the guiding channel; rejected because it makes every finding homework and is the weak outcome `Q-007`'s stress test was written to avoid. *Treating a rulebook's restatement of an external instrument as groundable* — rejected in Context; a restatement is the firm's own document. *The checker rewriting the section* — rejected in decision 3.

**Open — not decided here.** **Severity's vocabulary** (how many levels, and whether severity is the checker's judgement or a per-organisation configuration) is deliberately left to the schema ticket, since it is a naming question rather than a design fork. **Whether findings persist against a document across redrafts** — a finding cleared in v0.3 and reintroduced in v0.5 — is a lifecycle question that belongs with the review cycle, not here. **Whether the checker runs automatically at freeze or promotion**, rather than only on demand, is a workflow-placement question for the declared-workflow set (`Q-006`). ~~**Whether open findings block promotion**~~ — ✅ **answered the same day by [ADR-0049](0049-notifications-and-the-promotion-gate.md) §7**, as this ADR asked: it **blocks**, identically to an open comment thread, overridable only on `Participant.elevated` and audited. One obligation lands back here — promotion must be able to **count** open findings, which is a query the finding schema (T-328) must support.
