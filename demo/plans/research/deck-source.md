## The workspace's qualities (deck slide 12)

1. **Governed:** construction is a legal industry; one system must hold the record.
2. **Unified:** context is scattered across every system; it must live in one place, structured for AI.
3. **Multiplayer:** projects run on many firms; sharing must be the default, not the friction.
4. **Mouldable:** no two projects work alike; teams shape their tools, they aren't sold a workflow.

"No more round trips - no downloading to analyse, no pasting to draft. AI works
where the work is: inside the record, with the project's context."

## The solution line (slide 11)

"One collaborative workspace - a shared context engine to manage the record,
keep teams aligned, and tooling to help teams build the application layer they need."

## Language rules that apply to on-screen copy

- Never say "operating system". The wedge is the **collaborative workspace**;
  the vision is the **delivery system**.
- The villain is the **tooling**, never the paperwork. The record is legitimate
  and critical.
- Say "information" when speaking as the industry (document management).
  Say "context" when speaking about what AI does with it (context layer).
- "Context engine" is reserved for the solution and Context Layer slides.
- No competitor or vendor names on screen.
- **Hyphens, not em dashes.**
- Use only evidenced numbers; the walkthroughs make no numeric claims.
